## Delivery areas and timing
We deliver across Lebanon and the wider MENA region, including major cities
such as Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Riyadh, Doha, Amman, and Cairo. Standard delivery
takes 2–4 business days domestically, 5–8 business days internationally.
Express delivery (next-day, Beirut only) is available at checkout.

## Order tracking
Every shipment gets a tracking number sent by email and SMS once it
leaves our facility. Tracking updates in real time on our website.

## Lost or delayed packages
If a package is more than 3 days late, contact support with your
tracking number. We open an investigation with the carrier and issue
a refund or reshipment if the package can't be located within 5 days.

## Damaged items
Report damage within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We'll arrange
a replacement or full refund — no need to return the damaged item.
