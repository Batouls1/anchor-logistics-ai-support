## Cancellations
You can cancel an order within 1 hour of placing it, free of charge, from
Order History on our website or app. After that window, contact support
with your order number — we'll cancel it at no charge if it hasn't entered
processing yet. Once an order has started processing or shipped, a
cancellation fee may apply, or the order may no longer be cancellable; in
that case you can return it after delivery under our standard return
policy instead.

## Missed delivery
If you're not available when the courier arrives, they'll leave a delivery
attempt notice and try again the next business day. After 3 failed
delivery attempts, the order is returned to our warehouse and you'll be
contacted by support to arrange redelivery or a refund. You can also
request a specific delivery window or an alternate pickup point through
Order History before the first attempt.

## Customs, duties, and international orders
For international deliveries outside Lebanon, any customs duties, import
taxes, or clearance fees charged by the destination country are the
customer's responsibility and are not included in the order total at
checkout. These are collected by the local carrier or customs authority
on delivery. Delivery times for international orders may be extended if
a shipment is held for customs clearance.
